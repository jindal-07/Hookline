# Episode Script Output Format (Reference)

**This template shows the standard episode script structure**

**❌ Never output**:

- Frontmatter metadata
- Template instructions
- "Next steps" instructions
- Any non-content notes

**✅ Output only**: the actual script content

---

## Final output format:

```markdown
# Episode {XX}: {Title}

> **Series continuity note**: {Briefly explain how this episode connects to earlier ones, 1-2 sentences}

## Main Characters

- **{Character name}**: {Short description - canonical appearance from first appearance}
- **{Character name}**: {Short description}

## Scene 1 - {INT./EXT.} {Location} - {Time}

{Scene-establishing description - 2-3 visual sentences}

{CHARACTER NAME} ({age}, {key appearance}) {action description}.

{Dialogue or continued action}

---

## Scene 2 - {INT./EXT.} {Location} - {Time}

{Scene description}

{Character action and dialogue}

---

[Continue with scenes 3-N...]

---

## Key Beat Candidates

(**Note**: This section is for the Storyboard Artist's reference only; optional in the actual output)

1. **Beat 1** (Scene X) - {Brief description of the key moment}
2. **Beat 2** (Scene X) - {Brief description}
   ...
3. **Beat 9** (Scene X) - {Brief description}
```

## Example:

```markdown
# Episode 02: The Rift

> **Series continuity note**: Picks up right after ep01 - having found the diary, Emma enters the otherworldly library for the first time.

## Main Characters

- **Emma**: 25, straight waist-length silver hair, pale skin, violet eyes, long black trench coat over a white turtleneck
- **The Library Guardian**: age unknown, a glowing incorporeal form in a long dark blue robe

## Scene 1 - INT. Emma's Apartment - Early Morning

A tiny studio apartment, sunlight filtering in through grimy windows. The walls are covered in sticky notes and photos.

EMMA sits on the edge of the bed, gripping the leather diary. Her eyes are bloodshot; she clearly hasn't slept all night.

The diary flips open on its own to the page with the hexagram, giving off a faint golden glow.

EMMA
(whispering to herself)
This is impossible...

She reaches out and touches the pattern. Instantly, every object in the room begins to float.

---

## Scene 2 - INT. Void Passage - Time Is Meaningless

Emma is surrounded by streams of violet-blue energy, floating in an endless void. Her black trench coat billows as if underwater.

Fragments of reality fly past her - the corner of a subway platform, the shadow of an unfamiliar building.

The diary glows against her chest, forming a protective golden sphere of light.

EMMA's expression shifts from fear to focus. She begins to understand this is not a dream.

---

[Continue...]

## Key Beat Candidates

1. **Beat 1** (Scene 1) - The diary activates on its own; objects in the room float
2. **Beat 2** (Scene 2) - Emma travels through the void passage
3. **Beat 3** (Scene 3) - Arrives at the otherworldly library
4. **Beat 4** (Scene 5) - First meeting with the Library Guardian
5. **Beat 5** (Scene 7) - Discovers her family's secret
6. **Beat 6** (Scene 9) - The Guardian tests Emma's will
7. **Beat 7** (Scene 11) - Emma passes the test
8. **Beat 8** (Scene 13) - Gains her first magical ability
9. **Beat 9** (Scene 15) - Decides to accept her fate and returns to the real world to prepare for what comes next
```

## Key Points:

1. **Scene heading** - must include number, location type, location and time
2. **First appearance** - a character's first appearance must include a full appearance description
3. **Visual** - every description is concrete and visual
4. **Beat candidates** - at least 9 clear key moments
5. **No metadata** - output contains no frontmatter or notes
